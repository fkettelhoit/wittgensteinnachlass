The transcriptions on this website use the remark numbers established by the Bergen Nachlass Edition of the University of Bergen and use the facsimiles of Wittgenstein’s Nachlass made available in the Bergen Nachlass Edition by the Wren Library, Trinity College, Cambridge, the Austrian National Library, and the University of Bergen. The transcriptions on this website largely correspond to the Bergen Nachlass Edition but are more heavily normalized and in some cases rewritten. Most of the graphics come from the Wittgenstein Nachlass Graphics project and are released under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Wittgenstein’s Nachlass is in the public domain in countries where a copyright term of death of the author + 70 years applies (except where other regulations apply, such as the British 2039 rule).
