Wittgenstein’s Writings

This website provides transcriptions and facsimiles of Ludwig Wittgenstein’s writings from 1914 to 1951 in a normalized reading edition. It is not a diplomatic edition: Line breaks, underlining, and textual variants have been simplified. It is meant to complement the work of the Wittgenstein Archives Bergen, which maintain the authoritative edition of Wittgenstein’s entire Nachlass.
