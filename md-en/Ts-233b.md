### [Z](/w-z/#ts-233b-8.5.2) <span class="fac">[8\[5\].2](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ts-233b/8.webp)</span> {#8-5-2}

“Knowledge,” however, is not usually used in this sense. “I know how much 97 × 78 is.” “I know that 97 × 78 is 432.” In the first case, I tell someone that I can do something, that I possess something; in the second, I simply assert that 97 × 78 is 432.
