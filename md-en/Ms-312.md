Song to Filù

Lovely is the quail’s call

Lovely bulbul’s sweet lament

But Filù’s songs of delight

Fill your heart with blissful trembling

Tender is moss on the forest floor

Zephyr’s gentle, busy rustling

More tender Filù’s woollen threads

When they curl beneath the foot

Enduring is the oak’s trunk

quartz’s mountain-towered rising

_Eternal_ Filù’s webwork

Which bears the step on wings

_Ludwig Wittgenstein_

22.01.1925
