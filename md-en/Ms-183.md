# Ms-183

### <span class="fac">[1\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/1.webp),[2\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/2.webp),[3\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/3.webp),[4\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/4.webp)</span>

April 26, 1930

Without some courage, one cannot even write a sensible remark about oneself. I sometimes believe I suffer from a kind of mental constipation. Or is it just an illusion similar to when one feels like vomiting, when in fact there is nothing left inside? I am very often, or almost always, full of anxiety. My brain is very irritable. Today I received handkerchiefs from Marguerite for my birthday. They pleased me, even if I would have preferred every word from her, and a kiss even more. Of all the people who are alive now, her loss would affect me the most; I do not say this lightly, for I love her, or I hope that I love her. I am tired and lacking in ideas, which of course is always the case in the first few days after my arrival until I get used to the climate. But of course, it is not certain that I am not facing a period of emptiness altogether. It always terrifies me when I think about how my entire profession depends on a gift that can be taken away from me at any moment. I think very often, again and again, about this, and in general about how everything can be taken away from one, and one doesn't even know what one has, and one only becomes aware of the most essential things when one suddenly loses them. And one doesn't notice it precisely because it is so essential, and therefore so commonplace. Just as one doesn't notice that one is constantly breathing until one gets bronchitis and sees that what one took for granted is not so self-evident. And there are many more kinds of mental bronchitis. Often I feel that there is something in me, like a lump that, if it were to melt, would make me cry, or I would then find the right words (or perhaps even a melody). But this something (is it the heart?) feels like leather in me and cannot melt. Or is it that I am just too cowardly to let the temperature rise enough?

### <span class="fac">[4\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/4.webp)</span>

There are people who are too weak to break. I am one of them.

### <span class="fac">[4\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/4.webp)</span>

The only thing that might break me, and which I sometimes fear, is my mind.

### <span class="fac">[4\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/4.webp)</span>

I sometimes think that my brain will not be able to withstand the strain and will give way. For it is terribly strained for its strength – at least that is how it seems to me often.

### <span class="fac">[4\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/4.webp),[5\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/5.webp)</span>

27.04.1930

Until about my 23<sup>rd</sup> year, it would have been impossible for me to sleep in a freestanding bed, and I also only wanted to sleep with my face towards the wall. I don’t know when this fear left me. Was it only during the war?

### <span class="fac">[5\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/5.webp),[6\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/6.webp)</span>

A few days ago, I dreamed the following: I was leading a mule, of which I seemed to be the caretaker. First on a street – I think in an oriental city; then into an office where I had to wait in a large room. In front of this was a smaller room with many people. The mule was restless and stubborn. I held it on a short leash and thought that I would like it to run its head against the wall – the one I was sitting next to – then it would become calmer. I kept talking to it and called it “Inspector” at the same time. And indeed, this seemed to me to be the usual name for a mule, just as one calls a horse “Brown” or a pig “Wuz”. And I thought, “when I come to horses now, I will also call them Inspector” (i.e., I am so used to using the word “Inspector” in connection with mules). When I woke up, it only occurred to me that mules are not actually called “Inspector.”

### <span class="fac">[6\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/6.webp),[7\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/7.webp),[8\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/8.webp)</span>

Ramsey’s mind was very unpleasant to me. When I came to Cambridge 15 months ago, I thought I wouldn’t be able to associate with him, because I remembered our last meeting about 4 years ago at Keynes’s in Sussex so unfavorably. Keynes, to whom I said this, told me that I should be able to talk to him quite well, and not just about logic. And Keynes’s opinion was confirmed. Because I was able to communicate quite well with Ramsey about many things. But in the long run, it didn’t really work out. Ramsey’s inability to have genuine enthusiasm or genuine reverence, which is the same thing, finally disgusted me more and more. On the other hand, I had a certain apprehension about Ramsey. He was a very quick and skillful critic when presented with ideas. But his criticism did not help, but rather stopped and sobered up. The short period, as Schopenhauer calls it, between the two long periods in which a truth appears paradoxical to people, and then trivial, was reduced to a point in Ramsey. And so, one would try for a long time in vain to make something clear to him until he suddenly shrugged his shoulders and said that it was self-evident. But he was not dishonest. He had an ugly _mind_. But no ugly soul. He really enjoyed music, and with understanding. And you could see what effect it had on him. He told me that when he heard the last sentence of one of the last Beethoven quartets, which he loved more than perhaps anything else, he felt as if the heavens were open. And that meant something from him.

### <span class="fac">[9\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/9.webp)</span>

Freud is certainly often wrong, and as far as his character is concerned, he is probably a pig or something similar, but there is a great deal in what he says. And the same is true of me. There is a lot in what I say.

### <span class="fac">[9\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/9.webp)</span>

I like to dawdle. Perhaps not as much as in the past.

### <span class="fac">[9\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/9.webp),[10\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/10.webp)</span>

28.04.1930

I often think that the highest thing I would like to achieve would be to compose a melody. Or I am surprised that I have never come up with one when I have this desire. But then I have to tell myself that it is probably impossible for me to come up with one, because I lack something essential or _the_ essential thing. That is why it hovers before me as such a high ideal, because then I could, as it were, summarize my life; and crystallize it. And even if it were only a small, shabby crystal, but still one.

### <span class="fac">[10\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/10.webp),[11\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/11.webp)</span>

April 29, 1930

I am only truly happy when, in a certain sense, I am enthusiastic. And then I am again afraid of the collapse of this enthusiasm. Today, Mrs. Moore showed me a stupid critique of a performance of Bruckner's 4<sup>th</sup> Symphony, in which the critic rails against Bruckner and also speaks disrespectfully of Brahms and Wagner. At first, it didn't make an impression on me, because it is natural that everything – great and small – is barked at by dogs. Then, it did cause me pain. In a certain sense, I feel touched (strangely) when I think that the mind will never be understood.

### <span class="fac">[11\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/11.webp),[12\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/12.webp)</span>

30.04.1930

Unfruitful and sluggish. As for the mental: I always think to myself: did these great people suffer so terribly, that today some idiot comes along and gives his opinion about them. This thought often fills me with a kind of hopelessness. – Yesterday I sat for a while in the garden of Trinity, and I thought how strangely the good physical development of all these people goes together with complete lack of spirit (I don’t mean lack of intellect). And how, on the other hand, a theme by Brahms is full of strength, grace, and swing, and he himself had a belly. In contrast, the spirit of today has no springs under its feet.

I would just like to eat and sleep all day. It is as if my mind is tired. But of what? I have not done anything real in all these days. I feel stupid and cowardly.

### <span class="fac">[12\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/12.webp),[13\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/13.webp)</span>

01.05.1930

It takes extraordinarily long for something to become clear to me. – This is true in many different areas. For example, my relationship with other people only becomes clear to me after a long time. It’s as if it takes an incredibly long time for the huge cloud to dissipate and the object itself to become visible. But during this time, I am not even fully conscious of my lack of clarity. And suddenly, I see how things really were. That is why I would probably be useless in any situation where relatively quick decisions need to be made. I am, so to speak, somewhat blinded for a while, and only then does the scales fall from my eyes.

### <span class="fac">[13\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/13.webp),[14\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/14.webp)</span>

02.05.1930

In my lectures, I often try to win the favor of my audience by using a somewhat unusual approach; to entertain them so that they will willingly listen to me. That is certainly something bad. I often suffer from the thought of how much the success or the value of what I do depends on my disposition. More than in the case of a concert singer. Nothing is, as it were, stored in me; almost everything must be produced in the moment. This is – I believe – a very unusual way of working or living.

### <span class="fac">[14\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/14.webp),[15\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/15.webp)</span>

Since I am very weak, I am extremely dependent on the opinion of others. At least in the moment of acting. Unless I have had a long time to prepare myself. A kind word that someone says to me or a friendly smile has a long, pleasant, encouraging, and reassuring effect on me, and an unpleasant, i.e., unfriendly, word has an equally long, depressing effect. The most beneficial thing is then being alone in my room, where I restore the balance. At least the mental balance, although the nerves still retain the impression.

### <span class="fac">[15\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/15.webp)</span>

The best state for me is that of enthusiasm, because it at least partially consumes and neutralizes the ridiculous thoughts.

### <span class="fac">[15\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/15.webp),[16\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/16.webp)</span>

Everything, or almost everything, that I do, including these entries, is tinged with vanity, and the best thing I can do is to separate, isolate the vanity, and do the right thing despite it, even though it is always watching. I cannot drive it away. It is only sometimes absent.

### <span class="fac">[16\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/16.webp)</span>

I love Marguerite very much and am very afraid that she might not be well, as I have not received a letter from her for over a week. When I am alone, I think of her again and again, but also otherwise. If I were more decent, my love for her would also be more decent. And yet, I love her now as much as I can. Perhaps I am not lacking in intensity. But in decency.

### <span class="fac">[16\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/16.webp),[17\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/17.webp)</span>

06.05.1930

I am reading Spengler, _The Decline_, etc., and despite the many irresponsible things in it, I find many genuine, significant thoughts. Much of it, perhaps most of it, touches on what I myself have often thought. The possibility of a number of separate systems, which, once one has them, seem as if one is the continuation of the other. And this is also connected with the thought that we do not even know (consider) how much can be taken from – or even given to – a person.

### <span class="fac">[17\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/17.webp),[18\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/18.webp),[19\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/19.webp)</span>

Recently, I happened to read in Buddenbrooks about typhus & how Hanno B., in his final illness, was no longer recognized by anyone except a friend. & it occurred to me that one generally takes this for granted & thinks, of course, if the brain is once so disrupted, then it is only natural. But in reality, it is not the norm that we see people & do not _recognize_ them, but what we call “recognition” is only a specific ability that we could very well lose without being considered inferior. I mean: it seems obvious to me that we recognize people & that it is a complete disruption when someone does not recognize them. But it is quite possible that this stone is missing from the building without there being any talk of disruption. (This thought is again closely related to the Freudian ones, with the ones about the errors.)

### <span class="fac">[19\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/19.webp)</span>

That is, we take everything that we _have_ for granted and do not even know that we can be complete even without this and that, which we do not even recognize as a special ability because it seems to belong to the completeness of our mind.

### <span class="fac">[19\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/19.webp),[20\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/20.webp)</span>

It is a pity that Spengler did not stick to his good thoughts & went further than he could justify. However, with greater clarity, his thought would have been more difficult to understand, but also more truly and lastingly effective. Thus, the thought that string instruments assumed their _final_ shape between 15–1600 is of _immense_ significance (& symbolism). Only, when most people are given such a thought without much context, they see nothing in it. It is as if one believed that a person always develops infinitely & one said to him: look, the fontanelles of a child close after … years & that already shows you that development everywhere comes to an end, that what develops is a closed whole that will once be completely there & not a sausage that can continue indefinitely.

### <span class="fac">[21\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/21.webp)</span>

When I had the thought 16 years ago that the law of causality was meaningless in itself & that there was a way of looking at the world that did not take it into account, I had the feeling of the dawn of a new era.

### <span class="fac">[21\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/21.webp),[22\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/22.webp)</span>

In a relationship, I must be a very modern person because the cinema has an extraordinarily beneficial effect on me. I cannot imagine a way for the mind to rest that would be more appropriate than an American film. What I see & the music give me a blissful sensation, perhaps in an infantile sense, but that does not make it any less strong. In general, as I have often thought & said, the film is something very similar to the dream & Freudian methods can be directly applied to it.

### <span class="fac">[22\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/22.webp)</span>

A discovery is neither great nor small; it depends on what it means to us.

### <span class="fac">[22\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/22.webp),[23\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/23.webp),[24\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/24.webp)</span>

We see in the Copernican discovery something _great_ – because we know that it _meant_ something great to its time & perhaps also because a lingering echo of this meaning still reaches us – & now we conclude, by analogy, that the discoveries of Einstein etc. are at least something equally great. But they are – even if they are of great practical value, of diverse interest etc. – only as _great_ as they are _significant_ (symbolic). It is, therefore, _naturally_ similar to – for example – heroism. A feat of arms from earlier times is – rightly – praised as a heroic deed. But it is quite possible that an equally difficult or even more difficult feat of arms today is merely a sporting event & undeservedly receives the name “heroic deed”. The difficulty, the practical significance, all of that can, as it were, be judged from the outside; the _greatness_ of the heroism is determined by the _meaning_ that the action has, by the pathos that is associated with the way of acting. Because, however, a specific period of time connects a specific race with its pathos in very specific ways of acting, people are misled & believe that the greatness, the significance necessarily lies in that way of acting. And this belief is only ever refuted when, through a change, a revaluation of values takes place, i.e. the true pathos now turns to other ways of acting. Then – probably always – the old, now worthless tokens will continue to circulate for some time & be passed off by not entirely honest people as something great & significant, until one finds the new insight trivial again & says “_naturally_ these old tokens are worth nothing”.

### <span class="fac">[24\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/24.webp)</span>

### <span class="fac">[25\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/25.webp),[26\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/26.webp),[27\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/27.webp)</span>

09.05.1930

I am very much in love with Respinger, indeed I have been for some time, but now even more so. However, I also know that the situation is, in all probability, hopeless. That is to say, I must be prepared for the fact that she may become engaged and marry at any moment. And I know that this will be _very_ painful for me. So I know that I should not devote all my energy to this, because I know that it will eventually fail. That is to say, I should keep both feet firmly on the ground and only hold onto the rope, but not hang on it. But that is difficult. It is difficult to love so selflessly that one holds onto the love but does not want to be held by it. – It is difficult to hold onto the love in such a way that, if things go wrong, one does not have to see it as a lost game, but can say: I was prepared for this, and that is also okay. One could say: “If you do not get on the horse, i.e. entrust yourself to it completely, then of course you will never be thrown off, but you will also never hope to ride.” And one can only say to that: you must devote yourself completely to the horse, but also be prepared for the fact that you may be thrown off at any time.

### <span class="fac">[27\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/27.webp)</span>

In reality, one can only write down – that is, without doing something silly and inappropriate – what arises in us in the form of writing. Everything else seems strange and as if it were dirt. That is to say, something that ought to be wiped away.

I believe that today it takes heroism to see things _not_ as symbols in the Krausian sense. That is to say, to free oneself from a symbolism that can become routine. This does not, of course, mean trying to see them flat again, but rather to evaporate the clouds of, as it were, cheap symbolism in a higher sphere (so that the air becomes transparent again). It is difficult not to give in to this symbolism today.

### <span class="fac">[31\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/31.webp),[32\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/32.webp),[33\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/33.webp),[34\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/34.webp),[35\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/35.webp),[36\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/36.webp),[36\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/36.webp),[37\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/37.webp),[38\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/38.webp),[39\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/39.webp),[40\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/40.webp)</span>

02.10.1930

Arrived in Cambridge. Left Vienna on the 26<sup>th</sup> and went to Aunt Clara in Thumersbach, and although it wasn't as wonderfully pleasant there as usual in Laxenburg, it was still nice, and I left with good feelings. On the evening of the 27<sup>th</sup>, I arrived in Gottlieben, and it was tense there, as so much remained unresolved, and we sat in the car in which she picked me up, and at dinner we were either silent or talking about trivial things. And speaking haltingly, or flowing in a forced manner, as one does when one is actually burdened with serious matters. After the meal, I began to talk about her last letter. I said that I found a certain tone of triumph in it to be inappropriate. That if everything were in order, she would not have written in a triumphal tone, because she would then also have seen the difficulties and accepted the pleasant things as a grace from heaven. I asked her to come to Vienna as soon as possible and work there. Only when we (especially I) had already talked quite a lot did I see that she was very unhappy. Basically, the thought of getting married was dominant in her. It seemed to be the only real solution for her. _That is what she needs_, and nothing else. I asked her to be patient. The right thing – the appropriate thing – would be found for her. She should now primarily focus on working properly again and wait for the rest. Only when she is engaged in proper work will everything become clearer and easier for her. – During this conversation, she was again quite distant from me, she rather avoided my kisses, and she often looked downright sullen and looked away, which I had never seen in her before, and which rather frightened me. She seemed cold towards me, bitter, and unhappy, and almost rejecting. The next morning, it was a little better. We went for a walk and chatted a bit, and she was more approachable and affectionate. She was now determined to go to Vienna and seemed generally calmer. But in the evening, after another serious conversation, she began to cry. I held her in my arms, and she cried on my shoulder. After a few hours of not very intense work, I can no longer think. It is as if I were tired now. Is there a lack of proper nourishment? It is possible.

### <span class="fac">[232\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/232.webp),[233\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/233.webp),[234\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/234.webp)</span>

04/06/1937

An interpretation of the Christian doctrine: Wake up completely! If you do that, you will recognize that you are no good; & with that, the joy in this world will cease for you. And it cannot return if you remain awake. But now you need _redemption_, – otherwise you are lost. But you must stay alive (and this world is dead for you), so you need a new light from elsewhere. In this light, there can be no cleverness or wisdom; for this world, you are dead. (For it is the paradise in which, because of your sinfulness, you cannot do anything.) You must therefore acknowledge yourself as dead, & embrace a _different_ life (for without that, it is impossible to acknowledge yourself as dead without despair). This life must, as it were, keep you suspended above this earth; i.e., when you walk on the earth, you no longer _rest_ on the earth, but _hang_ in heaven; you are held from _above_, not supported from below. – But this life is love, human love, for the Perfect. And _this_ is faith. “Everything else will be found.” Thank God that today I am clearer & I feel better. Today I noticed again how quickly I become depressed when people, for whatever reason, are not _very_, not _particularly_ friendly to me. I asked myself: why am I so disgruntled about it? & I answered myself: “Because I am completely insecure.” Then the _comparison_ occurred to me that I feel just like a _bad_ rider on a horse: if the horse is in a good mood, then it goes well, but as soon as the horse becomes restless, he becomes insecure, he _notices_ his insecurity & that he is completely dependent on the horse. So, I think, it is also the case with my sister Helene with people. So one is inclined to think of people as good at one time & bad at another, depending on whether they are more or less friendly to him at the moment.

The ice is now melting and I have to cross the river by boat. This brings inconveniences and (slight) dangers. I am easily discouraged and anxious. ### <span class="fac">[236\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/236.webp)</span>

The brevity of an expression: the brevity of an expression cannot be measured with a ruler. Some expressions are _shorter_, even though they are _longer_ on paper. It is easier to write an ‘f’ _like this_: <math display="inline"><mtext>ƒ</mtext></math>, than _like this_: <math display="inline"><mo>∫</mo></math>. One often feels that a sentence is too long & then one wants to shorten it by removing words; as a result, it acquires a forced & unsatisfactory brevity. 16.04.1937

Since yesterday, the birches have had small green shoots. – I have been feeling somewhat unwell for a few days now, and also _very_ tired. I am not working well, although I am trying. I am not clear how much sense it makes to stay here for another 14 days. One voice tells me: ‘why not leave earlier!’ & another says: wait & stay! – If only I knew what was right! In the last few days, I have often read “Emperor & Galilean,” and with great impression. – There are many reasons to leave; but also cowardice. And there is also something for staying – but also pedantry, fear of the judgment of others, and so on. – It is not right to run away, to give in to impatience & cowardice, and on the other hand, it seems unreasonable, and also cowardly, to stay here. If I stay here, I am afraid of getting sick & then not getting home & to England: as if I could not get sick or have an accident in Vienna, etc.! _It is_ harder to stay here than to leave.

### <span class="fac">[238\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/238.webp),[239\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-183/239.webp)</span>

19.04.1937

I believe: through the word “believe” in religion, an awful lot of harm has been done. All those complicated thoughts about the ‘paradox,’ the _eternal_ meaning of a _historical_ fact, and so on. But if you say instead of “believe in Christ”: “love for Christ,” then the paradox disappears, i.e., the irritation of the _intellect_. What does religion have to do with such tickling of the intellect? (This, too, can be part of one’s religion.) It is not that one can now say: yes, now everything is simple – or understandable. It is not at all _understandable_; it is only not _ununderstandable_. –

Tonight, early in the morning, almost all the ice on the lake was driven down the river, so that the lake suddenly became almost completely free. For a few months now, I have been bleeding again during bowel movements & also have _some_ pain. – I often think that I might die of colon cancer. However it may be – may I _die_ well! I have definitely decided to leave here on May 1st – God willing.
