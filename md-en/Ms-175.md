# Ms-175

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-1r.1) <span class="fac">[1r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1r.webp)</span> {#1r1}

What does it mean to say that the truth of a sentence is _certain_?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-1r.2+1r.3+1v.1) <span class="fac">[1r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1r.webp),[1r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1r.webp),[1v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1v.webp)</span> {#1r21r31v1}

With the word “certain,” we express complete conviction, the absence of all doubt, & we use it to convince others. That is _subjective_ certainty. But when is something objectively certain? – When error is impossible. But what kind of possibility is that? Must not error be _logically_ excluded?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-1r.2+1r.3+1v.1.2) <span class="fac">[1r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1r.webp),[1r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1r.webp),[1v\[1\].2](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1v.webp)</span> {#1r21r31v12}

If I believe I am sitting in my room & it is not the case, then one would not say that I was _mistaken_: But what is the essential difference between a mistake & this case?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-1v.3+2r.1) <span class="fac">[1v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/1v.webp),[2r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2r.webp)</span> {#1v32r1}

Certain evidence is that which we accept as absolutely certain, according to which we act with certainty without doubt. What we call “error” plays a very specific role in our language-games, & so does what we consider to be certain evidence.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-2r.2) <span class="fac">[2r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2r.webp)</span> {#2r2}

It would be wrong to say that we consider something as secure evidence because it is certainly true.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-2r.3+2v.1) <span class="fac">[2r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2r.webp),[2v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2v.webp)</span> {#2r32v1}

Rather, we must first determine the role of making a decision for or against a sentence.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-2v.2) <span class="fac">[2v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2v.webp)</span> {#2v2}

The use of “true or false” has something misleading about it, because it is as if one said “it agrees with the facts or not,” and yet the question is precisely what “agreement” is here.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-2v.3+3r.1) <span class="fac">[2v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/2v.webp),[3r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/3r.webp)</span> {#2v33r1}

“The sentence is true or false” actually only means that a decision for or against it must be possible. But that does not say, on what grounds one decides for or against it.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-3r.2) <span class="fac">[3r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/3r.webp)</span> {#3r2}

Imagine someone asking: “Is it really true that we rely on the evidence of our memory (or our senses) as we do?”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-3v.1) <span class="fac">[3v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/3v.webp)</span> {#3v1}

Moore’s certain sentences almost say that we have a right to rely on this evidence.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-3v.2) <span class="fac">[3v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/3v.webp)</span> {#3v2}

What we call historical evidence suggests that the Earth existed long before my birth. The opposite hypothesis has _no_ support.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-3v.3+4r.1) <span class="fac">[3v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/3v.webp),[4r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/4r.webp)</span> {#3v34r1}

A hypothesis for which everything speaks in favor, against which nothing speaks – is it then objectively true? One can _call_ it that. But does it _unconditionally_ agree with the facts? At best, it shows us what “agree” means. We find it difficult to imagine it as false, but also difficult to make an application of it. – – – With this question, you are already moving in a circle.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-4v.1) <span class="fac">[4v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/4v.webp)</span> {#4v1}

What is this agreement, if not that what is evidence in this language-game speaks in favor of our sentence? (Log. Phil. Abh.)

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-4v.2+5r.1) <span class="fac">[4v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/4v.webp),[5r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5r.webp)</span> {#4v25r1}

But the justification of the evidence comes to an end; but the end is not that certain sentences immediately appear to us as true, that is, a kind of _seeing_ on our part, but our _action_, which lies at the basis of the language-game.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-5r.2) <span class="fac">[5r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5r.webp)</span> {#5r2}

If the true is the justified, then the ground is not _true_, nor false.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-5r.3+5v.1) <span class="fac">[5r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5r.webp),[5v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5v.webp)</span> {#5r35v1}

If someone asked us “But is that _true_?”, we could say to him “Yes”; and if he demanded reasons, we could say “I cannot give you any reasons, but if you learn more, you will also be of this opinion.” If he did not come to that, it would mean that he, for example, cannot learn history.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-5v.2) <span class="fac">[5v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5v.webp)</span> {#5v2}

“Strange coincidence that all the people whose heads one has looked into had a brain!”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-5v.3+6r.1) <span class="fac">[5v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/5v.webp),[6r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6r.webp)</span> {#5v36r1}

I have a telephone conversation with New York. My friend tells me that his little tree has the so-and-so buds. I am now convinced that the little tree is … Am I also convinced that the Earth exists?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-6r.2) <span class="fac">[6r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6r.webp)</span> {#6r2}

The fact that the Earth exists is rather part of the whole _picture_, which is the _basis_ of my belief.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-6r.3) <span class="fac">[6r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6r.webp)</span> {#6r3}

Does my telephone conversation with New York confirm my conviction that the Earth exists?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-6r.4+6v.1) <span class="fac">[6r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6r.webp),[6v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6v.webp)</span> {#6r46v1}

Some things seem to us to be established and they drop out of consideration. They are, as it were, put on a siding.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-6v.2+7r.1) <span class="fac">[6v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/6v.webp),[7r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7r.webp)</span> {#6v27r1}

It is now the form of our way of looking at things and characterizes all our questions and investigations. It may once have been disputed. Perhaps, however, it has belonged to the _framework_ of all our considerations for time immemorial. (Every human being has parents.)

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-7r.2) <span class="fac">[7r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7r.webp)</span> {#7r2}

For example, we consider a calculation to be sufficiently checked under certain circumstances. What gives us the right to do so? Experience? Could it not deceive us? We must end justifying somewhere, and then the sentence remains: that we _so_ calculate.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-7r.3+7v.1) <span class="fac">[7r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7r.webp),[7v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7v.webp)</span> {#7r37v1}

Our ‘experience sentences’ do not form a homogeneous mass.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-7v.2) <span class="fac">[7v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7v.webp)</span> {#7v2}

What is wrong with the idea that this table, when no one is looking at it, either disappears or changes its shape & color, and only returns to its old state when someone looks at it? – “Who would even assume such a thing!” – one might say.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-7v.3+8r.1) <span class="fac">[7v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/7v.webp),[8r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8r.webp)</span> {#7v38r1}

Here it becomes clear that the concept of ‘agreement with reality’ is not entirely clear.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-8r.2) <span class="fac">[8r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8r.webp)</span> {#8r2}

The sentence “It is written.”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-8r.3+8v.1) <span class="fac">[8r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8r.webp),[8v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8v.webp)</span> {#8r38v1}

Who would assume that _all_ our calculations are unreliable, & that we cannot rely on any of them (with the justification that errors are possible everywhere), we would perhaps declare to be mad. But can we say that he is mistaken? Is he not simply reacting differently: we rely on them, he does not; we are certain, he is not.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-8v.2) <span class="fac">[8v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8v.webp)</span> {#8v2}

Can I, for a moment, believe that I have ever been in the stratosphere? No. That is how I _know_ the contrary – like Moore?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-8v.3+9r.1) <span class="fac">[8v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/8v.webp),[9r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9r.webp)</span> {#8v39r1}

There can be no doubt about this for me, as a reasonable person. – That is what it is. –

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-9r.2) <span class="fac">[9r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9r.webp)</span> {#9r2}

A reasonable person has certain doubts _not_.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-9r.3) <span class="fac">[9r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9r.webp)</span> {#9r3}

Can I doubt what I _want_ to doubt?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-9r.4) <span class="fac">[9r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9r.webp)</span> {#9r4}

I cannot possibly doubt that I was never in the stratosphere. Do I know it because of that, is that why it is true?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-9v.1) <span class="fac">[9v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9v.webp)</span> {#9v1}

Could it not be that I am simply crazy and do not doubt what I absolutely should doubt.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-9v.2+10r.1) <span class="fac">[9v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/9v.webp),[10r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10r.webp)</span> {#9v210r1}

“I _know_ that it never happened, because if it had happened, I would have been unable to forget it.” But suppose it did happen, then you would have forgotten it. And how do you know that you could not possibly have forgotten it? Not just from past experience?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10r.2) <span class="fac">[10r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10r.webp)</span> {#10r2}

What I hold on to is not _a_ sentence, but a nest of sentences.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10r.3) <span class="fac">[10r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10r.webp)</span> {#10r3}

Can I subject the assumption that I was once on the moon to serious consideration at all?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10r.4) <span class="fac">[10r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10r.webp)</span> {#10r4}

“Is this something that one can forget?!”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10r.5+10v.1) <span class="fac">[10r\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10r.webp),[10v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10v.webp)</span> {#10r510v1}

“Under such circumstances, people do not say: ‘Perhaps we have all forgotten it’ and so on, but they assume…”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10v.2) <span class="fac">[10v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10v.webp)</span> {#10v2}

Our language acquires its sense through our other actions.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-10v.3+11r.1) <span class="fac">[10v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/10v.webp),[11r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/11r.webp)</span> {#10v311r1}

We ask ourselves: What do we do with the statement “I _know_…?” Because we are not concerned with processes or states of the mind. And _that_ is how one must decide whether something is knowledge or not.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-11r.2) <span class="fac">[11r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/11r.webp)</span> {#11r2}

If someone doubted whether the Earth existed 100 years ago, I wouldn’t understand _why_, because I wouldn’t know what he would still consider evidence, & what he wouldn’t.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-11r.3+11v.1) <span class="fac">[11r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/11r.webp),[11v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/11v.webp)</span> {#11r311v1}

“Each of these facts we could doubt, but we cannot doubt _all_ of them.” Would it not be more correct to say: “We do not doubt _all_ of them.” The fact that we do not doubt them all is precisely the way we judge, i.e., act.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-11v.2+12r.1) <span class="fac">[11v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/11v.webp),[12r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/12r.webp)</span> {#11v212r1}

If a child asked me whether the Earth existed before my birth, I would not answer that the Earth only came into existence with my birth, but that it existed long, long before. And in doing so, I would have the feeling of saying something strange. Something like when the child asked whether this or that mountain is higher than a very tall building that it has seen. I could only answer that question to someone to whom I would first impart a worldview. If I now answer the question with certainty, what gives me this certainty?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-12r.2+12v.1+13r.1) <span class="fac">[12r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/12r.webp),[12v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/12v.webp),[13r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/13r.webp)</span> {#12r212v113r1}

I believe that I have ancestors and that every human being has them. I believe that there are different cities and, in general, I believe in the main data of geography and history. I believe that the Earth is a body on whose surface we move and that it will not suddenly disappear or anything of the kind, like any other solid body: this table, this house, this tree, etc. If I wanted to doubt the existence of the Earth long before my birth, I would have to doubt everything that is certain to me.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-13r.2) <span class="fac">[13r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/13r.webp)</span> {#13r2}

And the fact that something is certain to me has its reason not in my stupidity or credulity.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-13r.3) <span class="fac">[13r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/13r.webp)</span> {#13r3}

If someone said “The Earth has not existed for a long time…” – what would he be touching upon? Do I know it?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-13v.1) <span class="fac">[13v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/13v.webp)</span> {#13v1}

Must it be a kind of scientific belief? Could it not be a mystical one? Must it necessarily contradict historical facts? Yes, even geographical ones?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-13v.2+14r.1+14v.1) <span class="fac">[13v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/13v.webp),[14r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/14r.webp),[14v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/14v.webp)</span> {#13v214r114v1}

If I say, “This table didn’t exist an hour ago,” I probably mean that it was only made later. If I say, “This mountain didn’t exist back then,” I presumably mean that it only formed later, perhaps volcanically. If I say, “This mountain didn’t exist half an hour ago,” that is such an odd statement that it is not clear what I mean. For example, whether I mean something false, but scientific. Perhaps one means that the statement that the mountain did not exist at that time is quite clear, however one imagines the connection. But imagine someone said, “This mountain didn’t exist a minute ago, but rather a perfectly identical one.” Only the familiar surroundings make it appear clear what is meant.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-14v.2+15r.1) <span class="fac">[14v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/14v.webp),[15r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/15r.webp)</span> {#14v215r1}

So I could ask the person who claims that the Earth did not exist before his birth further questions in order to find out which of my opinions he contradicts. And it _might_ be that he contradicts my fundamental views. And if that were the case, then I would have to stop there. Similarly, if he says that he once was on the moon.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-15r.2+15v.1+16r.1) <span class="fac">[15r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/15r.webp),[15v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/15v.webp),[16r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/16r.webp)</span> {#15r215v116r1}

Yes, I believe that every human being has two human parents; but Catholics believe that Jesus only had one human mother. And others might believe that there are people who have no parents, and who do not believe in any contrary evidence. Catholics also believe that a wafer completely changes its nature under certain circumstances, and at the same time, that all evidence proves the opposite. So if M. said, “I know that this is wine and not blood,” Catholics would contradict him.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-16r.2+16v.1) <span class="fac">[16r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/16r.webp),[16v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/16v.webp)</span> {#16r216v1}

What is the basis of the belief that all people have parents? Experience. And how can I base this firm belief on my experience? Well, I don’t just base it on the fact that I knew the parents of certain people, but on everything I have learned about the sex life of people, and their anatomy and physiology; also on what I have heard and seen about animals. But is that really proof?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-16v.2) <span class="fac">[16v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/16v.webp)</span> {#16v2}

Isn’t this a hypothesis which, as I _believe_, is always completely confirmed?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-16v.3) <span class="fac">[16v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/16v.webp)</span> {#16v3}

Shouldn’t we say, at every turn:

“I _believe_ this with certainty”?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-17r.1) <span class="fac">[17r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/17r.webp)</span> {#17r1}

“I know…” one says when one is ready to give compelling reasons. “I know” refers to a possibility of interpreting the truth. Whether someone knows something can be shown, assuming that he is convinced of it. But if what he believes is of such a nature that the reasons he can give are no more certain than his assertion, then he cannot say that he knows what he believes.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-17v.1) <span class="fac">[17v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/17v.webp)</span> {#17v1}

If someone says, “I have a body,” one can ask him, “Who is speaking here with this mouth?”

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-17v.2+18r.1+18v.1) <span class="fac">[17v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/17v.webp),[18r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/18r.webp),[18v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/18v.webp)</span> {#17v218r118v1}

To whom does one say that one knows something? To oneself, or to another. If one says it to oneself, how does it differ from stating that one is _certain_ that it is so? There is no subjective certainty that I know something. Subjective is the certainty, but not the knowledge. If I therefore say “I know that I have two hands” & that is not just supposed to express my subjective certainty, then I must be able to prove to myself that I am right. But I cannot; for the fact that I have two hands is no less certain before I have looked at them than afterwards.

However, I could say: “The fact that I have two hands is an unshakable belief.” That would express that I am not prepared to accept anything as a counter-proof of this sentence.

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-18v.2) <span class="fac">[18v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/18v.webp)</span> {#18v2}

“Here I have arrived at a basis for all my knowledge.” “I will _maintain_ this position!” But isn’t that precisely because I am completely _convinced_ of it? – What does it mean to ‘be completely convinced’?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-75v.2+76r.1+76v.1) <span class="fac">[75v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/75v.webp),[76r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/76r.webp),[76v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/76v.webp)</span> {#75v276r176v1}

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-77r.2+77v.1) <span class="fac">[77r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/77r.webp),[77v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/77v.webp)</span> {#77r277v1}

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-77v.2+78r.1) <span class="fac">[77v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/77v.webp),[78r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/78r.webp)</span> {#77v278r1}

I say “I know p” either to assure that the truth p is also known to me, or simply as an intensification of ⊢p. One also says “I do not _believe_ it, I _know_ it.” And one could also express it like this (e.g.): “That is a tree. And that is not merely a conjecture.” But what about this: “If I were to communicate to someone that that is a tree, it would not be a mere conjecture.” Is this not what Moore wanted to say?

### [OC](/w-oc/#ms-175-78r.2+78v.1) <span class="fac">[78r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/78r.webp),[78v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-175/78v.webp)</span> {#78r278v1}

It would not be a conjecture, & I could communicate it to the other with absolute certainty, as something that cannot be doubted. But does that mean that it is necessarily the truth? Can what I recognize with the greatest certainty as the tree that I have seen here all my life, can it not turn out to be something else? Can it not surprise me? And yet it was right, under the circumstances that give this sentence sense, to say “I know (I do not just conjecture) that that is a tree.” In truth, to say that I only believe it would be wrong. It would be completely _misleading_ to say: I believe that I am called L.W. And it is also true: I cannot be _wrong_ about that. But that does not mean that I am infallible in it.
