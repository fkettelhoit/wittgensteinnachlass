25.09.1940

For about 6 months now, my life has been highly unsatisfactory. For over 6 months, my work has stagnated & my mind is mostly dull, except that it is sometimes filled with _very_ general ideas. I feel physically & mentally aged. I would like to write something down: about myself, or about something else. – Because of my inability to work, my position here has become a burden to me (although I haven't pointed this out to anyone), but I am unclear within myself about what I should do; whether I should give up my position & try to find another kind of work. My life is heading towards a desert.

## Philosophical Remarks

### <span class="fac">[1r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1r.webp)</span>

26.09.1940

Can one say that the concept of ‘following a rule’ is acquired through experiments?

### <span class="fac">[1r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1r.webp)</span>

27.09.1940

‘The identity of 25 × 25 and 625 is established by experiment no more than that of 25 and 25.’

### <span class="fac">[1r\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1r.webp),[1v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1v.webp)</span>

Describing something as a regularity does not force us to do so by the facts. – Just as we are not forced to describe anything at all.

### <span class="fac">[1v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1v.webp)</span>

Who says that we must describe; & who says that we must calculate?

### <span class="fac">[1v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1v.webp)</span>

‘Why shouldn’t one take the calculation as the _basis_ of the description, without it being an experiment?’ But what does that mean?

### <span class="fac">[1v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/1v.webp)</span>

Is the result of the calculation that I was inclined to calculate in such and such a way?

### <span class="fac">[2r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/2r.webp)</span>

29.09.1940

Can we not imagine a language-game in which the calculation serves the description, but is never described? It is described by means of it, but it is not described but only executed. –

### <span class="fac">[2r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/2r.webp)</span>

The kind of ‘description’ is not accountable to any fact. It can appear completely absurd.

### <span class="fac">[2r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/2r.webp),[2v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/2v.webp)</span>

I want to say: Performing a calculation is only an experiment if (or is no more an experiment than) naming a thing, a color, for example.

### <span class="fac">[2v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/2v.webp),[3r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3r.webp)</span>

Can one say: When one measures, one is not trying to find out something about the measuring instrument? – And how does it show that when one calculates, one does not want to find out something about the calculator? But I want to say: Not because one then makes certain _assumptions_ (or allows them to apply). – I do not call something ‘green’ because I _assume_, presuppose, that my naming will agree with that of other people. I do not base my judgment on assumptions about the functioning of language.

### <span class="fac">[3r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3r.webp)</span>

All these remarks are weak & their language _wavers_.

### <span class="fac">[3r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3r.webp)</span>

I read, for example, something written, transfer the writing into sounds: I do not conduct an experiment; I am not curious about how I might pronounce the written _a_. Nor do I _presuppose_ that I will pronounce it the same way as everyone else.

### <span class="fac">[3r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3r.webp),[3v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3v.webp)</span>

30.09.1940

To say that mathematics is based on experience would be like saying that the usefulness of experience is based on experience. Or: one thinks that because thinking has proven useful.

### <span class="fac">[3v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3v.webp)</span>

I feel more dead than alive. I have the feeling of a nasty end of life.

### <span class="fac">[3v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/3v.webp),[4r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/4r.webp)</span>

01.10.1940

How I have lost the ability to investigate, I do not know myself. What was once an overviewable landscape is now an unmanageable one for me. As if I had become a small crawling creature.

### <span class="fac">[4r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/4r.webp),[4v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/4v.webp)</span>

Let us assume I describe a language-game in which someone has been taught to perform additions in the decimal system in order to, say, add up numbers in front of people & find out how many loaves of bread he should bake for everyone. How do I describe to you what he learns & does? I say what rules he learns & what exercises he does, & that he then follows the rule. How do I say that? Something like: “& in this way he proceeds, whatever numbers one gives him” or I say at a certain point “& so on”. But this means that in this description of his doing, I myself use a rule & its application.

### <span class="fac">[4v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/4v.webp),[5r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/5r.webp)</span>

If I now describe what he does when he calculates according to that instruction, then I am not only describing what state he puts himself in & how he perhaps recalls the expression of the rules in his memory, but in each individual case I can also describe what partial results & what final result he obtains when he _follows_ the instructions.

### <span class="fac">[5r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/5r.webp)</span>

‘Following the rule’ means at each stage: making a step that can be described in detail.

### <span class="fac">[5r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/5r.webp),[5v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/5v.webp)</span>

Therefore—I want to say—following the rule is not an experiment; because I cannot be eager (to know) _what_ I will now write, if the rule, for example, says I should write a series of identical digits & I have just written a 3. But of course there is a mistake here as well.

### <span class="fac">[5v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/5v.webp),[6r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/6r.webp)</span>

October 2, 1940

I prepare myself to follow a certain rule: Is it now a result of experience that, following this rule, I write down this and that? It is, of course, a result of experience that, _after these preparations_, I write down this and that. Or also: that, believing I am following the rule, I write down this and that.

### <span class="fac">[6r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/6r.webp)</span>

Is it a result of experience that _this_ is a consequence of the rule? Is it a result of experience that we now call _this_ color ‘green’?

### <span class="fac">[6r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/6r.webp),[6v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/6v.webp)</span>

Could I also describe the language-game from earlier in this way: – Someone has been trained to proceed according to a rule: & now he conducts experiments by setting himself the task in question & seeing _what_ he considers a solution to the task –? – Is that the language-game from earlier? – Previously, I described not only how he prepared to act according to the rule, but also what he does in each individual case. So if what he does is an _experiment_, I used to say not only what kind of experiments he conducts, but also what the results of these experiments are.

### <span class="fac">[6v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/6v.webp),[7r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/7r.webp)</span>

Can I now say that the one who follows the rule checks what way of acting he will consider to be in accordance with the rule? Or does he also check what he is inclined to understand in that moment by “in accordance with” or “correct”?

### <span class="fac">[7r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/7r.webp)</span>

03.10.1940

‘He puts himself in the right situation, lets it happen, and then acts according to the result of this happening.’ Is that correct? (I think it is correct; and yet something is wrong in my understanding.)

### <span class="fac">[7v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/7v.webp)</span>

Mathematicians cannot philosophize about mathematics for this reason, because they are too afraid that the legitimacy of their work might be called into question. They only want to secure their reasoning as quickly as possible. If they had more _faith_, they could take more time.

### <span class="fac">[7v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/7v.webp)</span>

Are we more certain that we understand what it means for someone to have written the multiplication ‘6460 × 3213’ than that he has carried it out according to the rules?

### <span class="fac">[8r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8r.webp)</span>

Anyone to whom I have said that someone has performed this multiplication correctly will be able to write down this _starting point_ with the same certainty as the entire multiplication.

### <span class="fac">[8r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8r.webp)</span>

“He follows this rule” does not mean: “he investigates what happens when he intends to follow the rule.” At most: “he conducts the experiment … and the result is that he follows the rule.”

### <span class="fac">[8r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8r.webp),[8v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8v.webp)</span>

04.10.1940

If my talent leaves me – what can I do?! Am I lost?

### <span class="fac">[8v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8v.webp)</span>

### <span class="fac">[8v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8v.webp)</span>

To think, without reservation, the opposite of what one believes, is difficult.

### <span class="fac">[8v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8v.webp)</span>

I want to say: Every description uses a frame of reference. A rule and its applications belong to the frame of reference.

### <span class="fac">[8v\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/8v.webp),[9r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9r.webp)</span>

Why do we accompany events with noise (speech)? Not every noise is language; it must be organized in such and such a way.

### <span class="fac">[9r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9r.webp)</span>

The result of an experiment is described in a rule.

### <span class="fac">[9r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9r.webp)</span>

‘We do not need to make hocus pocus; we only want to describe what actually happens.’ But that is not so easy for the scientific mind. Because it finds it difficult to say that it has no practical reason for something.

### <span class="fac">[9v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9v.webp)</span>

What if a person no longer understands what it means: “proceed in this way”?

### <span class="fac">[9v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9v.webp)</span>

One can certainly conduct experiments to determine what someone’s understanding of the word ‘green’ is. – But the answer may, under certain circumstances, be that he understands _green_ by it.

### <span class="fac">[9v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/9v.webp)</span>

The danger in our explanations is that they are not deep enough. But they are not deep enough if we overlook something.

### <span class="fac">[10r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/10r.webp)</span>

October 7, 1940

It is practical to introduce the concept of a rule in a language-game in which someone, upon being _commanded_ to follow a rule—such as performing a calculation—follows the rule.

### <span class="fac">[10r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/10r.webp)</span>

I spent the _whole_ day preoccupied with thoughts about my relationship with Kirk. For the most part_, very_ misguided and fruitless. If I were to write these thoughts down, one would see how shallow and distorted my thoughts are.

### <span class="fac">[10v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/10v.webp)</span>

October 8, 1940

No one here would say that the person who obeys the command is conducting an experiment on himself—unless obeying a command is _always_ an experiment.

### <span class="fac">[10v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/10v.webp)</span>

What then determines whether he has followed the rule or not? If _he_ decides this, is it enough for him to say honestly that he has followed it? What then determines that he has achieved understanding of what it _means_ _to_ ‘follow the rule’?

### <span class="fac">[10v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/10v.webp),[11r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11r.webp)</span>

Is whether he follows the rule (follows it _in some way_) judged by whether he puts himself in a certain state? – Well, a matter of attention does play a role, however.

### <span class="fac">[11r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11r.webp),[11v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11v.webp)</span>

The _phenomenon_—I mean the ethnological phenomenon—of mathematics, and the features that can be understood as characteristics of this phenomenon, is very difficult to describe; in particular, the transitions (slopes) from characteristic mathematical actions to others.

### <span class="fac">[11v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11v.webp)</span>

The command to calculate can, of course, be given in the question: “What is … × …?”

### <span class="fac">[11v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11v.webp)</span>

“The result of the calculation may surprise us.” – Undoubtedly. But what follows from that? – Well, that it is a real, new fact!

### <span class="fac">[11v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/11v.webp),[12r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/12r.webp)</span>

What is it like when someone is surprised by the result of the calculation? Well, for example, like this: the factors of the multiplication are 777 and 77; the result contains no 7. He says: “That surprises me; I would have thought the result would contain at least _a_ ‘7’.”

### <span class="fac">[12r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/12r.webp),[12v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/12v.webp)</span>

What is the _physical_ content of the commutative law, for instance, as it relates to the multiplication of numbers in the decimal system? It does make a prediction here, or rather: _it allows_ _for_ a physical prediction. Therefore, there must (after all) be a physical law _related to_ this mathematical theorem!

### <span class="fac">[12v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/12v.webp)</span>

October 9, 1940

Insofar as the proof allows us to make a _prediction_ about the result of the calculation, it thus functions like a proof in physics, for example in the mechanics of writing. Or should I say: “mechanics of signs”??

### <span class="fac">[12v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/12v.webp),[13r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/13r.webp),[13v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/13v.webp)</span>

The calculation predicts something – – but what does it predict? That people who have learned to calculate will calculate in this way? Or: that people who have learned to calculate will only provide an explanation for those calculations that have this outcome? How is the proof to be understood as a prediction: for the result of all calculations, whether they are calculated correctly or incorrectly, or only for those calculated correctly? Well, the first case would indeed be remarkable, but that is not the one we mean. It is the second case we mean. We want to say: If we perform _all_ the steps of the calculation correctly, we will arrive at that result in the end. But couldn’t that mean different things? For example: if we take a step from a to b and now want to build on the result of that step, so that this result does not change _unnoticed_ on paper, and in this way we are led, seemingly logically, to the most diverse results.

### <span class="fac">[13v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/13v.webp),[14r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14r.webp)</span>

And if the mathematical proof can serve as the basis for a prediction, why not _just_ that? Why doesn’t that (then) constitute its essence?

### <span class="fac">[14r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14r.webp)</span>

10.10.1940

This leads to the example of the game of putting together & the template: – ‘Is the template a prediction that it will succeed in forming this figure with these stones?’ –

### <span class="fac">[14r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14r.webp)</span>

I would like to say: it is & it is not! –

### <span class="fac">[14r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14r.webp),[14v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14v.webp)</span>

One can certainly say: The geometric is _part_ of the physical here. But what characterizes this part as mathematical? The special method. –

### <span class="fac">[14v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/14v.webp)</span>

One can, after all, predict _with the help of proof_ that under normal circumstances people will only accept as correct those calculations whose conclusion is this result. If, for example, I calculate a multiplication, I can predict that a class of students, together with their teacher, will eventually all arrive at _the_ result. And of course I can also predict with the greatest certainty the intermediate results they will arrive at.

### <span class="fac">[15r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/15r.webp)</span>

It is very rare that one can predict human actions with such certainty.

### <span class="fac">[15r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/15r.webp)</span>

11.10.1940

So, if I said, ‘multiplication works like this and that,’ does that mean that almost all people with a certain education will calculate in the same way?

### <span class="fac">[15r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/15r.webp),[15v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/15v.webp)</span>

I want to look at the matter from the most sober, most commonplace angle. I can say: it is a fact that there is no _reliable_ construction (with ruler and compass) of the heptagon. And the mathematical proof of the ‘impossibility of the heptagon’s construction’ certainly shows us this as well.

The mathematical sentence never stands on three legs but on four. It is, as it were, overdetermined in comparison with an empirical proposition.

12.10.1940

### <span class="fac">[15v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/15v.webp)</span>

It is difficult to work with a knife in one’s body.

### <span class="fac">[16r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/16r.webp),[16v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/16v.webp)</span>

October 16, 1940

Spent the _whole_ day yesterday thinking about … Am I crazy? Perhaps; but what is there to do? – You could think _endlessly_ about this object, imagine possibilities, weigh them, compare the case with various others, discard the comparisons as worthless; you can prepare yourself for an encounter, for all its possibilities—and yet you don’t know what will happen. You don’t know whether you are preparing yourself or spoiling it. You want to say: “_No_ _amount of_ preparation is too precious to me in such a case.” But do you know that you haven’t yet reached the point where all reasonable preparation _ends_? Yet something _compels_ you to keep thinking about it—and it’s always possible to keep thinking about it, to imagine scenarios. —You must gain a foothold outside of it, or you’ll be carried away by the current and drown.

### <span class="fac">[16v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/16v.webp),[17r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/17r.webp)</span>

October 17, 1940

One can solve physical problems with the help of mathematics; but it now seems that mathematics itself already provides _physical_ answers of a very primitive kind. – And that seems to make the role of mathematics immediately understandable, – for everyone understands that one wants to obtain physical answers.

In what respect does performing a multiplication, for example, increase my knowledge? What do I know now that I didn't know before performing it? – I now know that I reacted to this task in _this_ way. I know with great certainty that others will react in exactly the same way & that I will do so myself. I also know, of course, that the paper held up to the calculation, that the marks remained, etc. Is that the increase in my _mathematical_ knowledge? I also know that if I had formed so many rows of so many beads & then counted them all, I would have arrived at _this_ number with almost complete certainty. Also, that this could have been done with white, red & blue beads. That I – as I could also express it – could find the result of my multiplication in many different ways. The mathematical proposition – I would like to say – must stand on four legs, not three, like an empirical proposition.

_Mathematics_ – I want to say – is only that, insofar as it is recognized as a figure, placed among the rules.

For the rule could indeed guide, i.e., cause one to act in such and such a way, but my action does not necessarily have to be sanctioned as an expression of the rule at this level.

Yes, if our memory, or the signs, were such that something different came out each time we calculated, then there would be no proof and no calculation here.

Counting could be simply reciting a series of words, which would not have the characteristic uses that it has with us and which give it its character for us. Yes, it could then take on a _different_ meaning if it were, for example, a kind of dance or a kind of music.

The point that the mathematical sentence has is not independent of its use.

Excuse nothing, blur nothing, see and say how it really is – but you must see what casts a new light on the facts.

### <span class="fac">[56r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/56r.webp)</span>

As if I said: “25 apples & 25 apples are 50 apples & that is not supposed to be a statement about the apples.” The punchline lies in the ‘_that is not supposed to_’.

### <span class="fac">[56r\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/56r.webp),[56v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/56v.webp)</span>

I could also say, instead of: ‘I am not describing a psychological process with it’: ‘I do not want to describe a psychological process with it’, or: ‘this is not supposed to describe a psychological process’.

### <span class="fac">[56v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/56v.webp)</span>

The point is that the course of the calculation _can_ describe a psychological process, but it does not necessarily do so.

### <span class="fac">[56v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/56v.webp)</span>

Even if people differed and always reacted differently to the rule and training, there would be sentences about the psychological process – but no calculation.

### <span class="fac">[57r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/57r.webp)</span>

A language-game: one trains someone to calculate, e.g., to multiply. When asked “What is … × …?”, he has to do the multiplication, but it also applies when he says the result. If he knows that 25 × 25 = 625, does he know that, when asked the question, he will write something down, at the end of which is ‘625’? Does he know that _he_, that _someone_, will react like that? One can only ‘know that 25 × 25 = 625’ within a use practiced by society.

### <span class="fac">[57r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/57r.webp),[57v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/57v.webp)</span>

The thought of mathematical reality. It is only a reflection of the use of calculations and the opposite of the idea that the mathematical sentence says something about a psychological process.

### <span class="fac">[57v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/57v.webp)</span>

The mathematical sentence can, in a certain sense, and in a certain relationship, not be verified by the psychological reaction.

### <span class="fac">[57v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/57v.webp),[58r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58r.webp)</span>

It does not have the description of the psychological reaction as its task – it has a different function. Yes, it could also fulfill a similar function even if the commonality of the psychological reactions were not fulfilled. Yes, even to the extent that the commonality must be fulfilled, the sentence does not have the task of describing it – it is based on it. Because it would _assert_ it, if its opposite asserted its opposite. It has a completely different function than the psychological sentence.

### <span class="fac">[58r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58r.webp),[58v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58v.webp)</span>

‘The mathematical proof must be clear.’ That is: it is a picture that one must not only recalculate, but also, with equal success, (must) be able to copy.

### <span class="fac">[58v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58v.webp)</span>

‘The proof must be clear’ means: the way in which the proof produces its result must be contained in a picture.

### <span class="fac">[58v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58v.webp)</span>

The same proof is the one that is the _copy_ of the other – even if it was not _copied_ from it.

### <span class="fac">[58v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/58v.webp)</span>

This is, of course, also expressed by the fact that one speaks of a ‘proof figure’.

### <span class="fac">[59r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59r.webp)</span>

The most misleading thing is bringing in psychological concepts: that I have to _agree_ with the steps of the proof, that the proof _convinces_ me, that I _believe_ the mathematical sentence, and so on.

### <span class="fac">[59r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59r.webp),[59v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59v.webp)</span>

02.06.1941

‘The proof convinces us of the truth of the sentence by generating the sentence according to certain rules.’ But take, for example, a tautology and, on the other hand, a sentence like ‘20 × 20 = 400’: does the conviction serve the same purposes in both cases?

### <span class="fac">[59v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59v.webp)</span>

Does this mean that the sentence, when accepted, serves the same purpose in both cases?

### <span class="fac">[59v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59v.webp)</span>

And is it important what I feel when I accept the sentence, whether I nod or what I say? Isn't it rather what is important what I _do_ with the sentence when it has been accepted? What use does its acceptance express?

### <span class="fac">[59v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/59v.webp),[60r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/60r.webp)</span>

One can say: the proof demonstrates an internal property of a structure (of a sentence, a number, etc.) and this can be important for a variety of reasons.

### <span class="fac">[60r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/60r.webp),[60v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/60v.webp)</span>

03.06.1941

The objection to a behavioristic expression for the sentences of immediate experience is _not_ that this expression deals with something other than experiences. Rather, it is that we actually play a different, somewhat different, game with the expressions of experiences than with the descriptions of behavior. It is not an objection that the expression deals with external behavior, because what it ‘deals with’ is not necessarily shown in the expressions and their ostensive definitions, but in the system of the use of the expressions. If someone says worriedly: “He is groaning terribly,” then one _can_ say that he is not talking about behavior.

### <span class="fac">[60v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/60v.webp)</span>

Imagine someone said: “He has what I have, namely –” (and pricks himself with a needle).

### <span class="fac">[60v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/60v.webp),[61r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/61r.webp)</span>

‘To imagine pain & to imagine a behaviour are two different processes!’ – When one imagines pain, one often produces some kind of sensation in the relevant part of the body.

### <span class="fac">[61r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/61r.webp),[61v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/61v.webp)</span>

If someone asks: What is the difference between imagining pain and imagining behaviour, I would provide an explanation: in one case, you imagine something painful, a sting, a feeling—let’s say in the mouth—in the other case, a posture or movement of the body. – It is strange, however, that when I actually imagine pain, I do not picture the other person in a painful position, but I myself _make_ a pained face.

### <span class="fac">[61v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/61v.webp)</span>

04.06.1941

If I say that the proof demonstrates an internal property, this means that it is understood (used) as a demonstration of an internal property.

### <span class="fac">[61v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/61v.webp),[62r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62r.webp)</span>

The proven sentence, which has the internal property, can have all sorts of points. And I would like to say: what the point of the sentence is, is shown in the fact that it has an affinity with certain non-mathematical sentences. Whether this, as generally stated, is nonsense, I do not know. – – –

### <span class="fac">[62r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62r.webp)</span>

I said that the mathematical proof is understood as a demonstration of an internal property. Perform a proof by folding a, say, square piece of paper. The result can be interpreted as an internal, but also as an external, property.

### <span class="fac">[62r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62r.webp),[62v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62v.webp)</span>

(When dealing with the mathematical sentence, I am inclined to speak of a sense in Frege's sense.)

### <span class="fac">[62v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62v.webp),[62v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/62v.webp),[63r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63r.webp)</span>

What does the person who claims that 25 × 25 = 625 assert? Well, that 25 × 25 = 625. But I want to ask further. The sentence says that something times something equals something. Now that is also a non-mathematical sentence form, and an example of its sense is, for example, that 3 times the area

![](../graphics/123,62v-1.svg)

equals the area

![](../graphics/123,62v-2.svg)

. However, the mathematical sentence of this form still has the same sense, and yet it is different; that is, it – as it were – alludes to that sense, although it has a different use. But the use is, of course, not without connection with this sense.

### <span class="fac">[63r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63r.webp)</span>

One could almost say: “The mathematical sentence 5 × 5 = 25 says, as it were, that something times something equals something.”

### <span class="fac">[63r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63r.webp),[63v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63v.webp)</span>

And for example also: “'p⊃q․p:⊃․q' says, as it were, that if this and this is the case, then this is the case.”

### <span class="fac">[63v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63v.webp)</span>

Take Goldbach's theorem – on what does it depend that we understand what it says? Why, on the use of its words & word form in other sentences! But on nothing else! It has not yet been proven – – – what makes it that we understand _this_ statement? Why, the same! –

### <span class="fac">[63v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/63v.webp),[64r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64r.webp)</span>

If it were now proven – would we know better than now what the words “the proof of Goldbach's theorem” mean? Or would we know it _differently_? Would these words then have a different meaning? Or is it, as if I wish for an apple, when I know just as well what the object of the wish is before I have it, and afterwards.

### <span class="fac">[64r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64r.webp)</span>

I want to say: the expression “the proof of the sentence…”, if the proof exists, is not a Russellian description.

### <span class="fac">[64r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64r.webp)</span>

The difference between the proven & unproven mathematical sentence is not the same as the difference between the verified & unverified physical sentence. That is: the difference in usability and use is not the same.

### <span class="fac">[64r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64r.webp),[64v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64v.webp)</span>

The proof places the sentence in the system. It is, of course, also placed in it by its word form. And in this double placement lies the problem.

### <span class="fac">[64v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64v.webp)</span>

One _could_ say that the second placement gives it sense (Frege), and the first gives it truth-value. But that is precisely what I do _not_ want to say. Or: that is precisely what seems to me to be the most misleading aspect.

### <span class="fac">[64v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/64v.webp),[65r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65r.webp)</span>

Because, roughly speaking, the ‘sense’ should be given to it by the way in which it would be found to be true. But there can be a proof of the sentence, even if there is nothing that one could call a ‘method of verification’.

### <span class="fac">[65r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65r.webp)</span>

Now why not say: If you want to know what sense Goldbach's theorem has, look at what the mathematicians who want to prove it want to prove – and if you want to see that, look at what they actually do, what _attempts_ they make to prove it.

### <span class="fac">[65r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65r.webp),[65v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65v.webp)</span>

For it is through these attempts that they also situate the statement. Even if they do not, so to speak, determine its place (entirely) _precisely_, they do determine it to a certain degree.

### <span class="fac">[65v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65v.webp)</span>

The mathematical sentence, if it is not proven, is – one could say – _the expression of a problem_. The sense is the _problem_.

### <span class="fac">[65v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/65v.webp)</span>

Does the mathematical sentence assert an internal relation? – It asserts what it asserts. It asserts what its proof proves, & its proof demonstrates an internal relation, & yet it would be incorrect to say that the mathematical sentence asserts an internal relation. Could one not rather say: it asserts a certain applicability?

### <span class="fac">[66r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66r.webp)</span>

It asserts, as it were, its sense, as its words seem to offer (suggest) to us.

### <span class="fac">[66r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66r.webp)</span>

What the proof proves is that the sentence is true: that we _have_ here an instrument for _this_ use.

### <span class="fac">[66r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66r.webp)</span>

The proof presents the sentence as an instrument suitable for this purpose.

### <span class="fac">[66r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66r.webp),[66v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66v.webp)</span>

05.06.1941

‘The mathematical sentence says something’ – & what it says will be shown by its use, the use of the signs that form it. But is the use only in mathematics, or also outside?!

### <span class="fac">[66v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66v.webp)</span>

‘To recognize the mathematical sentence as true’ is this a mental activity? And what is its use? If we have now recognized a sentence as true – what does that matter? Why should this mental act interest me? (Why more than the joy or displeasure when looking at the sentence?)

### <span class="fac">[66v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/66v.webp)</span>

The question is: what is the sentence, which I recognize as true, an instrument for?

### <span class="fac">[67r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/67r.webp)</span>

In that language-game – why shouldn’t I say that the one who has learned to multiply and then performs a multiplication, learns a new fact through it? And yet – _what_ is it?

### <span class="fac">[67r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/67r.webp),[67v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/67v.webp)</span>

That he has now acted in this way? that he will probably always act in this way? that others act in this way? – And has he also thought intensely enough about the rule? has he really acted _according to it_? – That this gives that? But is this an explanation of the sense of “gives”? Or _must_ I imagine the rule as an impersonal mechanism that only affects me, and through me? For that is what mathematicians would like to say. The rule is an abstract mechanism.

### <span class="fac">[67v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/67v.webp)</span>

Now, whoever says that, says above all that the mathematical sentences _should not_ deal with a mental or physical mechanism. (For whoever says it does not simply say something stupid, but some truth wrapped in a misunderstanding.)

### <span class="fac">[67v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/67v.webp),[68r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/68r.webp)</span>

Whoever is trained in this way knows what to do and answer when asked, if he does not want to receive a punishment. He learns from the calculation what he has to answer.

### <span class="fac">[68r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/68r.webp)</span>

Whoever performs the calculation – must he think that he is thereby receiving information?? Why not simply: that he is doing something, _producing_ something?

### <span class="fac">[68r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/68r.webp)</span>

One could say: The calculation tells me that the others calculate in this way – _if_ I ask myself how the others calculate.

But if I don't ask, then it doesn't tell me.

### <span class="fac">[68v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/68v.webp)</span>

‘Could it be imagined that these operations would yield something else?’ – One would like to say: No. Because: then they would simply not be these operations. Now, how must one conceive of them, so that the _image_ of how they yield this is precisely what we produce when calculating?

### <span class="fac">[68v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-123/68v.webp)</span>

The calculation can _produce_ a sentence without _communicating_ (it, or) what it says.

Can I imagine how one checkmates with a single pawn in chess?
