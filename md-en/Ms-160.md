### <span class="fac">[1r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/1r.webp)</span>

What does one mean by saying that the development of A differs from the development of B?

How are the words “the development of A differs from the development of B” used? There is the case where A differs from B at the n-th stage, or the case where A is…

### <span class="fac">[1r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/1r.webp),[1v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/1v.webp),[2r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/2r.webp)</span>

How does one use the expression: “the development A is in agreement with the development B” – or “is not in agreement”? Firstly: A is in agreement with B at the n-th position. Or: A is in agreement with B from the n<sup>th</sup> to the m<sup>th</sup> position – and so on. “A” & “B” stand for expressions of rules. Furthermore: A is in agreement with B at every second position. A is in agreement with B at every n²-th position n² is in agreement. This & similar things one could call a “continuous agreement”. Or: A is in agreement with B at all positions – – – or: at no position.

### <span class="fac">[2r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/2r.webp)</span>

It seems natural to say: If A & B agree at _all_ outer positions, then A agrees with B, and the disagreement is negligible.

Just as the purpose of the rules of chess is not to correspond to the nature of chess, but to the purpose of the game of chess.

### <span class="fac">[5r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/5r.webp)</span>

“What is a rule?” – – – What is it, then?

### <span class="fac">[5r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/5r.webp),[5v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/5v.webp)</span>

Do they command you something, – advise you, inform you that people have acted in this way? Well, don’t you want to look at how the sentences are actually used? Most people who buy such a game read the rules & play according to them. However, these same rules could form parts of commands, requests, reports.

### <span class="fac">[5v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/5v.webp),[6r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/6r.webp)</span>

Such a rule, however, could be part of a command (to act according to it) or part of a report (that it is being acted upon), etc. And the rule itself could also be used as a command, report, etc.

### <span class="fac">[6v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/6v.webp),[7r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/7r.webp)</span>

### [CE](/w-ce/#ms-160-7r.2) <span class="fac">[7r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/7r.webp)</span> {#7r2}

Language is used – whether it employs words for physical objects or for sense impressions.

### [CE](/w-ce/#ms-160-7r.3+7v.1) <span class="fac">[7r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/7r.webp),[7v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/7v.webp)</span> {#7r37v1}

But are not sense impressions what is immediately perceived? The immediate objects to which everything we say must ultimately refer? The image of the building blocks. Everything we build ultimately consists of these building blocks. But the image is false, since we do not oppose ‘these’ building blocks to others.

### [CE](/w-ce/#ms-160-7v.2) <span class="fac">[7v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/7v.webp)</span> {#7v2}

How do you know which object you have given the name to? Yes, how do you know that you have given any name to anything at all?

### <span class="fac">[8r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/8r.webp)</span>

But can I not give a name to what is immediately perceived? – Of course! But this can be a name in a different sense; that is, it can function in various ways: and the name of the physical object, for example, to which this sense impression may correspond, is also such a name.

### <span class="fac">[8v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/8v.webp)</span>

What we call “giving a name to what is immediately perceived” is _a special kind_ of giving signs that relate to what is perceived. But there is no ‘direct’ and ‘indirect’ in this matter, unless we call a needlessly cumbersome method of designation ‘indirect’.

### [CE](/w-ce/#ms-160-9r.1+9v.1) <span class="fac">[9r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/9r.webp),[9v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/9v.webp)</span> {#9r19v1}

The question here is not whether the name of the physical object designates one object and the name of the impression designates another object, as if one were to point successively to two different objects and say: “I mean this object, not this one.” The image of the different objects is used entirely incorrectly here. It is not that one is the name for the immediate object and the other for something else; rather, the two words are simply used differently. When explaining a name, we do not even point to the immediate object once and to the physical object another time.

### <span class="fac">[9v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/9v.webp),[10r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/10r.webp)</span>

If we want to talk about an immediate object, we can say that the name of the sense impression, _like_ the name of the physical object, refers to the immediate object.

### <span class="fac">[10r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/10r.webp),[10v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/10v.webp),[11r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/11r.webp)</span>

Must the entire value of money be defined by the value of commodities? Can one then say that money does not actually have value, but only commodities have value? Money is, after all, an object of use, an instrument of a technique of commerce. If this technique is useful and preferable to barter, in what sense can one speak of money as an empty schema? Is it not as if one were to call a plane something that only has indirect value because it is not a piece of furniture? It is true that we do not _eat_ money. Is trade with money only a more cumbersome form of barter, or can one do with it what is not possible with the latter?

### <span class="fac">[11r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/11r.webp)</span>

One cannot speak of a more indirect method of designation, because what intermediate links should the name of a physical object designate?

### <span class="fac">[11r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/11r.webp),[11v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/11v.webp)</span>

The name of the impression and the name of the physical object are names only through the rules of their use. If one considers one to be a more direct designation than the other, then it is only because of certain relationships in the modes of use that suggest such an image of the direct and indirect.

### <span class="fac">[11v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/11v.webp),[12r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/12r.webp),[12v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/12v.webp)</span>

A definition of the material object by sense images would be conceivable if one then provided for appropriate rules to ensure that what is defined does everything that the name of a physical object does now. In this way, we would obtain a very cumbersome and uninteresting method of designation. What is interesting, however, is to see how the mode of use of a name for a physical object differs from what one might initially consider to be a definition of such a name.

### <span class="fac">[12v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/12v.webp)</span>

The relationship between the use of names for physical objects and the names for impressions is highly interesting. A definition of the former by the latter would be highly artificial and uninteresting.

### <span class="fac">[12v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/12v.webp),[13r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/13r.webp),[13v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/13v.webp)</span>

But can’t I say that in one case I am merely naming the _foreground_ [so, after all, two objects] of what is perceived, while in the other case I am naming what is behind it? – But that is only an image: in reality, I don’t want to say that what is immediately perceived has a foreground and a body behind it.

It is true that we sometimes imagine a sense-datum as a kind of stage set, something that looks like a bed, and only the foreground is painted paper that gives us the illusion of a bed.

### <span class="fac">[13v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/13v.webp),[14r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/14r.webp)</span>

I know – one opens one’s eyes and makes a gesture of pointing. But has one thereby given a name? But why does one perform this ceremony? Well, this is what one does when one wants to memorize the meaning of a name. It is, therefore, a useful action; and it is only a ceremony when we philosophize.

### <span class="fac">[14r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/14r.webp),[14v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/14v.webp),[15r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/15r.webp),[15v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/15v.webp)</span>

“When we speak of a thing (table, chair, etc., etc.), are we not really speaking of sense-data; present, past, and future?” Well, one can say that. – – – But if one speaks of sense-data _in this way_ – what more does one want? “But must there not then be a more direct way of speaking of sense-data, since one apparently speaks of other objects here, naming objects other than the sense-data?” But who says that this is not precisely the way of ‘speaking of sense-data’? (How does one marry money? Not (by) marrying a rich woman?) “But there is certainly another way of speaking of sense-data!” – Of course – and you can certainly name this way: ‘speaking directly of sense-data’; but consider how much right there is in speaking here of direct and indirect expression. Only a superficial view presents us with something of the mediate and the immediate here.

In _one_ sense, I speak _of_ this and that thing – in another sense, I speak of sense-data. ‘Thing’ and ‘sense-datum’ do not stand in such a relationship to each other, as if speaking of one were more or less the same as speaking of the other (and one could say: ‘You have now spoken of things – now speak of what one (actually) sees and hears!’)

### <span class="fac">[15v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/15v.webp),[16r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/16r.webp)</span>

“You are acting as if you were speaking of _that_, but you are speaking of something else.” But this is how one speaks of sense-data.

### [CE](/w-ce/#ms-160-16r.2) <span class="fac">[16r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/16r.webp)</span> {#16r2}

“You are speaking of the object, but you actually want to speak of sense-data.” – I want to speak as I speak, and you can say that I am speaking of sense-data. Only, speaking of sense-data does not look like what you imagine.

### <span class="fac">[16v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/16v.webp),[17r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/17r.webp)</span>

We are always strongly tempted to want to say more than what we _know_. What we do not know about the use of our words is as interesting as what we know. I mean: the fact that we cannot, or cannot clearly, answer questions about the use of words should be as important to us as the answers we can give. Giving a definition is not more important than stating that we cannot give one.

### <span class="fac">[17r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/17r.webp)</span>

If we want to understand the use of our language as it is, we must also be interested in the questions to which we do not know how to give an answer.

I mean, why should I replace the name of the thing with _all_ its phenomena, and not with _one_, and leave the rest to the use of this picture.

### <span class="fac">[26v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/26v.webp),[27r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/27r.webp)</span>

I am not saying: “Don’t believe that the laws of logic must apply everywhere; it is quite possible that there is a case in which the law of the excluded middle does not apply” – as if this were an empirical proposition and one must be careful not to assume blindly that it is true! – Rather, I say: Become accustomed to seeing different techniques of sign use (i.e., of thinking). I do not want to eliminate a prejudice of opinion but of technique. For example, do not be afraid of a contradiction in principle.

### <span class="fac">[27r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/27r.webp),[27v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/27v.webp),[28r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/28r.webp)</span>

And this is connected with the following: Become clear about the uselessness of the ordinary conception of meaning, sense, and truth. (More on this later.) Do not think so much about the images that accompany a word, a sentence, but about its use in the language-game. Do not ask whether this sentence can ‘be thought’ and try to decide it through introspection; rather, ask how it can be used, and its use will provide you with the images and feelings.

### <span class="fac">[28r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/28r.webp),[28v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/28v.webp),[29r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/29r.webp)</span>

Are you not caught in a prejudice; could you not possibly proceed differently here? _Must_ you, for example, say that the diagonal number is different from all those by means of which it was formed? Are there not other ways in which we can recognize equality? Or also: are you fully conscious of the unusualness of this mathematical situation? Are you conscious _how_ far you have strayed from the original conditions of the customary technique? – Have you overlooked anything in the vicinity of a particular calculation? Can I not persuade you that, under the _changed_ circumstances, a contradiction should not be seen as a sign of a fatal logical malady of the calculus?

### <span class="fac">[29r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/29r.webp),[29v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/29v.webp),[30r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/30r.webp),[30v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/30v.webp)</span>

As if one were to say: “I can apparently, in some sense, _try_ to bring the right hand into alignment with the left, and try in vain: so _experience_ teaches me that this is not possible; and it is not a matter of logical, but of empirical, physical impossibility.” The answer is: The sentence: “it is impossible to bring the right and left hands into alignment” can be used as a geometrical, but also as a physical sentence. (Or, as one can also say – although this gives rise to somewhat misleading ideas: the sentence can be ‘_meant_’ geometrically or also physically.) If the former, then it is established that any apparent success of the ‘attempt’ is to be described as an illusion of the senses, whether it succeeds or not; indeed, that the process of which I said it is ‘in some sense’ an attempt is not to be regarded as a _physical_ attempt, i.e. its result is not to be further treated as that of a physical attempt. In the second case, one would not say to the person trying “but don’t you see that it is impossible!” – but would perhaps express the hope that it might succeed one day. (The clever people)

### <span class="fac">[30v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/30v.webp),[31r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/31r.webp)</span>

“He cannot only not do it, he cannot even try” means: I will not recognize any process as the success of the attempt, and therefore not as such an attempt.

### <span class="fac">[31r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/31r.webp),[31v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-160/31v.webp)</span>

“But there is indeed a difference between these ‘attempts’!” – Of course there is one; the one that the description of the two processes reveals. And this difference can help us to assign the two different roles.
