### <span class="fac">[3r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/3r.webp)</span>

“What does that mean, you don’t want to say that _you_ have something?” – I don’t want to say that _something_ is happening to me now. That something now has a relationship to this body that it didn’t have to another one.

### <span class="fac">[3v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/3v.webp)</span>

What do you actually know when you know that you feel this ‘aching’? Do you know something about yourself, about your body? Do you know that _it_ is complaining? “But I don’t doubt which body is complaining!” I doubt it only as much as I can.

### <span class="fac">[3v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/3v.webp)</span>

Why do I want to say: “You don’t know anything – you are expressing something”?

### <span class="fac">[4r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/4r.webp)</span>

You only know what you can objectively verify. You _can_ verify, for example, that this sensation “aching” _means_ something. One could say: “that _is_ ‘aching’” by giving the other person an electric shock.

### <span class="fac">[4r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/4r.webp)</span>

My problem is: what can one say against the application of the word “to know” in this case?

### <span class="fac">[4v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/4v.webp)</span>

One could say to someone who expresses his sensation in the words: “I know one who…” – you are using the word “I know” in a special way.

### <span class="fac">[4v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/4v.webp)</span>

He didn’t learn it that way, as normally the expression “I know…” is used. There was in this game…

### <span class="fac">[5r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/5r.webp),[5v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/5v.webp)</span>

Instead of saying: “one cannot,” say: “there is not in this game…” Instead of “one cannot castle in checkers”: “there is no castling in checkers.” Instead of: “I cannot point to my pain”: “there is no pointing to what one has in the language-game ‘I am in pain.’” Instead of “one cannot enumerate all cardinal numbers,” there is no enumeration of all series members in the game “enumerating cardinal numbers.” ‘To know’ means to proceed according to certain criteria and to have arrived at a result.

### <span class="fac">[5v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/5v.webp)</span>

‘This and that knowledge’ means having arrived at this result.

### <span class="fac">[5v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/5v.webp)</span>

Knowledge is therefore controllable.

### <span class="fac">[5v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/5v.webp),[6r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6r.webp)</span>

What is the use of an expression of my knowledge that no one else can share with me? It would be like money that is of great value to the payer but not to the recipient.

### <span class="fac">[6r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6r.webp)</span>

I would like to say: every philosophical problem actually arises from a character flaw.

### <span class="fac">[6r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6r.webp)</span>

Knowledge can be confirmed.

### <span class="fac">[6r\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6r.webp)</span>

Knowledge is something that one can arrive at together.

### <span class="fac">[6r\[5\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6r.webp),[6v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6v.webp)</span>

An expression of knowledge is something that can be confirmed. An expression of knowledge can be checked for its correctness.

### <span class="fac">[6v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6v.webp)</span>

Knowledge is what, according to objective rules, can be proven to be true.

### <span class="fac">[6v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/6v.webp)</span>

An utterance of sensation can in some way be compared to the card that a player receives when dealing. It is a starting position of the game but not yet a result of the game.

### <span class="fac">[7r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/7r.webp)</span>

Logical _necessity_ shows itself in the fact that we build bridges and locomotives after calculations.

### <span class="fac">[7r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/7r.webp)</span>

Logical necessity is the necessity _to_ act according to thought.

### <span class="fac">[7r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/7r.webp),[7v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/7v.webp)</span>

How necessary – do I want to say – must it be to us if we follow it in the most important things?

### <span class="fac">[7v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/7v.webp)</span>

“I know one…” This does not have the character of a reference to a person, because it does not have the character of knowledge. – Because “I have…” could, however, be a reference to a person. Because one could not ask: show me how you are aching. Because there is nothing to show here.

### <span class="fac">[8r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/8r.webp)</span>

“I have…” is a reference to a person when it says something about my own body. In contrast, the expression of a sensation is not a communication about me; and the next communication is that I have made that utterance.

### <span class="fac">[8r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/8r.webp),[8v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/8v.webp)</span>

If you say: “I have pain,” then someone could answer you: show it if you have it. If you can’t show anything, then you don’t know anything either.

### <span class="fac">[8v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/8v.webp)</span>

“I know one who…” and you point to your body. True; but to what end? What can I do with your pointing? How can I use it? – Isn’t it as if you were saying: “It is beautiful outside” and pointing to yourself? You could also direct attention to your speaking mouth (want to).

### <span class="fac">[9r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/9r.webp)</span>

One could imagine that someone first raises his hand before saying it in order to direct the attention of the crowd to himself.

### <span class="fac">[9r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/9r.webp)</span>

Does a spoken something become a reference to a person by pointing to oneself while saying it?

### <span class="fac">[9r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/9r.webp),[9v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/9v.webp)</span>

Imagine one said: “I know one, namely me. Ouch!” But what _purpose_ do the first words serve?

### <span class="fac">[9v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/9v.webp)</span>

Suppose the game is this: the doctor has to look at the face of the person who says “I have pain” in order to see, as it were, whether he has hit the spot of the pain, and whether it is subsiding, etc. – Could one then say that the statement “I have pain” refers to a person; that I indicate to whom the doctor should look?

### <span class="fac">[10r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/10r.webp)</span>

The English: “I say!”

### <span class="fac">[10r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/10r.webp),[10v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/10v.webp)</span>

Suppose one said the words “I am speaking:” before every sentence, and its purpose would be to direct the other person’s attention to the speaker; or one clears one’s throat before each sentence for the same purpose, and the other person turns to the person clearing his throat. Is every sentence then a statement about the speaker? Or does anyone who says anything have to raise his hand.

### <span class="fac">[10v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/10v.webp)</span>

It is not about what I say to him; but about what he does with it.

### <span class="fac">[10v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/10v.webp)</span>

If one says that he feels the pain, then he is not also observing who has it. If one says that he sees the pain in the other, and feels it, then one cannot say “in himself”.

### <span class="fac">[11r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/11r.webp)</span>

One can say: Sometimes he observes pain in others, sometimes he feels pain; but not “in himself”.

### <span class="fac">[11r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/11r.webp)</span>

If I tell him that I have pain, then he does not proceed to investigate whether I really have it, and then perhaps to alleviate it.

### <span class="fac">[11r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/11r.webp),[11v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/11v.webp)</span>

How about the sentence: If one says: “I know that I see pain”, then the doctor has to look at the person the speaker is looking at; if, however, he says: “I know that I feel pain”, then he has to look at the speaker himself.

### <span class="fac">[11v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/11v.webp),[12r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/12r.webp)</span>

When I say: “I am speaking: …”, am I first telling him _who_ is speaking? If I now say: “_I_ often speak in similes,” then – I would like to say – I am informing him _who_ is doing this; but not when I say of a sentence: “I am speaking.” Why? In the first case, I would like to say that I _know_ something about myself; in the second, not.

### <span class="fac">[12r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/12r.webp)</span>

There is a certain sign, and if this is given, one must look at the one who gives it, whatever this sign itself is, whether it seems to point to him or to another.

### <span class="fac">[12r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/12r.webp),[12v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/12v.webp)</span>

The game was not that he learned to observe who has something specific, and that the other person then goes to the one or the other, according to my observation, which can also be checked. I did not learn it in such a way that my teacher and I observed the same thing. That I give this signal, which I have learned in this way, is decisive; not to whom I point while doing so.

### <span class="fac">[12v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/12v.webp),[13r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13r.webp)</span>

You _can_ say “I _know_ who has the pain precisely because I also have the words ‘I have’…” and use this as a signal. But if you now consider what you are referring to when you say that the reference to you is, as it were, becoming empty – – – I would like to say that the reference to me in the sensation signal is, as it were, becoming empty. What do I do with this allusion to myself? But I do use it. – But if it is not used as an allusion to my body, understood only _in that way_, it becomes empty.

### <span class="fac">[13r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13r.webp),[13v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13v.webp)</span>

“These words (whatever they are) always point to the person who says them.” But one does not then say that he is speaking about himself. One says “they point to him” when a direction has been established through observation.

### <span class="fac">[13v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13v.webp)</span>

It is true, they do not point to anyone else.

### <span class="fac">[13v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13v.webp)</span>

When he hears these words, he looks at the person who says them; he does not pay attention to whom he is pointing.

### <span class="fac">[13v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/13v.webp),[14r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/14r.webp)</span>

How is it with the words: “I tell you: – – –”? Do they point to a person other than me?

### <span class="fac">[14r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/14r.webp)</span>

My words do not tell him that a certain object is with me. He does pay attention to my face or turns to me with a question, but it is not about the fact that I have something.

### <span class="fac">[14r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/14r.webp),[14v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/14v.webp)</span>

Under what condition will I treat the other person’s words as a complaint of pain? Probably when they are accompanied by the other signs of pain in general. If that is the case, then I will act according to them as I would according to a complaint, whatever they seem to say.

### <span class="fac">[14v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/14v.webp)</span>

Suppose the complaint is: “Someone has pain” said in a complaining tone. But one could also replace the sentence “I have money” in the same way.

### <span class="fac">[15r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15r.webp)</span>

The complaint is the expression of the feeling of pain, not the fact that I have it now. I would like to say that when it speaks of pain, it does not also speak of me. But it only says: it does not say that I have something. Should I say I feel the pain now or that I have it?

### <span class="fac">[15r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15r.webp),[15v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15v.webp)</span>

Imagine you say, “This is the pain.” And now imagine the same pain, but if not you have it.

### <span class="fac">[15v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15v.webp)</span>

“The pain, when no one has it, is quite pleasant.”

### <span class="fac">[15v\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15v.webp)</span>

The complaint does not assert that my body has something; and therefore it cannot speak ‘of me’.

### <span class="fac">[15v\[4\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/15v.webp)</span>

The complaint does not speak of me because it does not speak of any other object.

### <span class="fac">[16r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/16r.webp)</span>

“I know that I have pain!” – You say it in any case, and I cannot want anything more; but the question is: what happens with this statement? What is its use?

### <span class="fac">[16r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/16r.webp),[16v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/16v.webp)</span>

We keep confusing statements of the kind: “I am more inclined to call this that than that” with statements: “This is this and not that.” All metaphysical statements could be made in the form: “I am not inclined to call this ‘sentence’,” “I am inclined to say that a relation ‘exists’ and not that it ‘subsists’,” “I am inclined to call the appearance of a chair an ‘object,’” etc., etc.

### <span class="fac">[16v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/16v.webp),[17r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/17r.webp)</span>

How could one decide whether, when you have pain, you have something? Well, you are inclined to use this expression; but is that the criterion that it is so? You can also use a gear as a paperweight. Then we would say: it doesn’t work as a gear here.

### <span class="fac">[17r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/17r.webp),[17v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/17v.webp)</span>

“I know who is feeling the pain now; namely, I.” This has something unsatisfactory. Why? If it is about the objective fact that something has a certain relationship to my body, then there is nothing unsatisfactory in the statement.

### <span class="fac">[17v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/17v.webp),[18r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/18r.webp)</span>

“I can now name someone who has that _feeling_; namely, _I_.” – Do I understand you correctly? Do you want to express a sensation? Or have you perhaps observed that your body twitches? You did say that you _know_ something. If I know that you have given the sensation signal, then you haven’t actually named anyone to me. The sensation signal doesn’t _name_ you in that sense, because it doesn’t connect you with anything else. I do turn to you as if you had named yourself. The sensation signal doesn’t even say “I,” or even “he,” in a stereotypical way. Therefore, if I know that what you say is a sensation signal, then the _I_ in it doesn’t tell me anything anymore.

### <span class="fac">[18r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/18r.webp),[18v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/18v.webp)</span>

I can imagine a pain expression in which the complainer raises his arm and points upward and says, “it hurts him, it hurts him!”

### <span class="fac">[18v\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/18v.webp)</span>

Don’t say “it works”; you work.

Dear Gilbert, I am sure you have been reminded of me several times in these last days reading the papers about Austria. As you can imagine I have been, & am, greatly troubled by the events. My relatives so far as I can judge are in no sort of danger as almost all of them are retiring & very respected people. My brother & sisters are, under the new laws, Jews (not however their children, as _they_ had no Jewish grandparents & baptized greatgrandparents). As you know I am automatically becoming a German citizen, i.e. a German Jew. A still graver consequence however is this that on visiting Austria I shall, in all likelihood, not be let out to England again. You understand of course that it is out of the question for me to get any job there but even if it _were_ possible I could hardly face such a possibility. You may call this weakness but such it is. My people are wealthyish & will probably even after all the changes have money enough to keep me. But I needn't say what that would mean for me. So I am now seriously considering the idea of acquiring British citizenship. You know that I had just _thought_ of this possibility before though never giving it any serious consideration for reasons which you & I have talked about. They roughly are, that I don't wish to become a sham-Englishman. The situation has however changed in my eyes now as I have to choose between a new nationality which deprives me of _everything_ & one which at least gives me the opportunity of working in a country in which I have lived most of my adult life have friends & have done my best work. I wish to God that there did not adhere to British nationality the respectability etc. etc. which (though it does not repel me) is _not_ what I seek. But this can't be helped. There is one enormously strong reason which would for me speak against acquiring a new nationality & it is this, that in _all_ likelihood as a British citizen I shall be barred by the Germans from re-entering Austria & therefore from seeing my family, except by meeting them, say, in Switzerland. But I don't see at present that this would be worse for me or them than my rotting alive in Austria or being haunted by a false position & anxiety in England. Please let me know _as soon as possible_ if, where & when you can meet me. Forgive me for making you read this _long_ letter please don't throw it away & read it again if you can. Whatever you may think about my problem I am

_always yours_

_bloodily_

_L._

### <span class="fac">[41r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/41r.webp),[41v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/41v.webp)</span>

‘God! I’m not making this utterance of pain unjustified! There is something there, which I mean!’ – But all of that fades away when I think that I always need a new intuition in order to tell myself what “pain” means.

### <span class="fac">[42r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/42r.webp)</span>

I want to say: “If I see it _this_ way, then the utterance of pain is justified – if I see it _this_ way, then it is not justified.” But _how_, & _how_? I say once _this_ once _that_ emphatically.

### <span class="fac">[42r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/42r.webp),[42v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/42v.webp)</span>

We say the sentences with _conviction_, because we can, in another context, actually assert them. The emphasis depends on the assertion that my utterance of pain is justified in a practical case; and from where does the emphasis of the assertion that the utterance is _not_ justified come? From the assertion that the utterance of pain has no and needs no justification. I.e.: “I can have pain, even if you don't perceive a cause!”

### <span class="fac">[44r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/44r.webp),[44v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/44v.webp)</span>

“It’s quite obvious to me that there’s something there corresponding to the word ‘pain’! Isn’t it obvious to you? –” – It is! But now let’s see what we can do with this statement. First of all: What difference does it make to the practical use of the word “pain”? Admittedly none! Then what should speak in favor of our judgment? Then isn’t it a queer sort of statement? I mean isn’t it a remarkable sort of statement if we have in mind that it’s the _use_ we make of a statement, the game that’s played with it in which usually its importance rests.

### <span class="fac">[45r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/45r.webp)</span>

If then we admit that it is an idle wheel in the mechanism of language, let’s turn it the _other_ way. It will turn, though perhaps a little more stiffly.

### <span class="fac">[45r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/45r.webp),[45v\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/45v.webp),[46r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/46r.webp)</span>

You say that it’s obvious to you that there’s something the word pain means. But you’re not satisfied, for in the next moment _you_ think that it might be any number of things corresponding to the word “pain,” & then again that there is not anything corresponding to the word “pain” but that what seemed to correspond to it was really part of the experience of saying it; etc.. In other words the pictures vary & replace one another, the language game however remains, only it doesn’t really seem to interest us. If it did we wouldn’t philosophize.

### <span class="fac">[46r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ms-158/46r.webp)</span>

“But isn’t it queer that I should wish to say such a sentence & feel as though it obviously makes sense?” – Not at all. The sentence makes sense all right. Only the way you use it, that you use it in a philosophical discussion, is queer.

“Then are you saying that a person learns how to use these words & then (just) says them, for no earthly reason?” – Exactly; there is no reason here of the kind you mean.
