# Ts-246

### <span class="fac">[1r\[1\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ts-246/1.webp),[1r\[2\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ts-246/1.webp),[1r\[3\]](https://cdn.wittgensteinnachlass.com/2000px/webp/Ts-246/1.webp)</span>

Do you cast over my head the fragrant veil of faithful love,

when the hands move

when the limbs gently stir

the soul is bereft of sensation.

Can you grasp it when it wafts,

moves quietly only

and deeply imprints its trace upon the heart.

When morning rings its bell

the gardener moves through the spaces of the garden

stirs his earth with light feet

and the flowers wake and look

questioningly into his bright,

calm countenance:

Who wove around your foot the veil

that touches us tenderly like the fanning of the wind

Is Zephyr himself in your service?

Was it the spider, was it the silkworm?
